Voice pipe canary

Raw mic capture into the real /ws/voice seam.

This page is the operator canary for the voice transport stack: getUserMedia, AudioWorklet, raw 16kHz PCM frames, /ws/voice, WhisperLive, buffer persistence, and commit into the bound tmux session. It is intentionally standalone and loud about its own failures.

Route /ws/voice?bind=<tmux_name>
Capture AudioWorklet • 16kHz PCM • mono
Cold start First press may take 5–10s

Control rail

bind · capture · commit

The selector uses /api/dao/active_sessions. If the target session is live but missing from the list, paste its tmux name manually and bind directly.

Flow: bind a session, grant mic access, then hold to talk. The first press sends start; later holds use unmute / mute. Reset ends the voice session, clears local readouts, and lets you rebind cleanly after upstream failures.
